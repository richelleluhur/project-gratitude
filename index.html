What we do


Showing appreciation to our school’s unseen heroes.

Project Gratitude is a service initiative dedicated to thanking our custodians and support staff, the people who work behind the scenes so the rest of us can get on with our day.

We want to foster a culture of gratitude where everyone’s contributions are recognised and valued, and where our community feels stronger because of it.

Why it matters

Most of us pass the same people every morning without knowing their names.

They line the field before a match, clear the tables at break, and fix things we never knew were broken. Many of them are also a long way from home.

We think noticing that, and saying so to their faces, is worth doing properly.

Mission · Vision · Values

Putting our Lasallian values into practice, not leaving them on a wall.

Mission

Inspire gratitude and connection.

Engaging students in meaningful acts of appreciation, honouring our custodians’ dedication and cultivating empathy among students. We want to move past a one-off “thank you” towards students who actually know the people they are thanking.

Vision

A community where everyone is seen.

A compassionate school community where every individual is valued and appreciated, including those whose contributions we encounter every day without always taking the time to know them.

What we aim to do

Build awareness & belonging.

Appreciate the people who keep SJII safe, clean and welcoming, strengthen connection between students and support staff, and draw attention to work that easily fades into the background.
